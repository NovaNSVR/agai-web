The question we hear most from creators considering AlphaGlow is: *how long does this actually take?*

The honest answer: your first session can be published within a day of getting access. Your full studio — with journeys, a Digital Twin, and custom soundscapes — takes a week of focused work. Here is what that looks like in practice.

## Day one: Application and onboarding

The application is straightforward. We ask about your area of expertise, your existing content (if any), and your audience. The review takes between one and three business days. When you are approved, you receive access to Nova Studio.

The first thing Nova asks you is what you want your program to achieve. Not what content you want to make — what outcome you want your listeners to reach. This shapes everything else.

## Days two and three: Building your first session

Sessions on AlphaGlow are guided audio or video experiences. If you already have recorded content, you can upload it directly. If you are starting fresh, Nova Studio gives you a script builder — you describe the session, Nova drafts a script, you refine it and record.

Most creators publish their first session on day two or three. The technical barrier is low. The harder work is knowing what the session is *for* and designing it to create a specific outcome.

## The rest of the first week: Journeys and structure

A journey is a sequence of sessions designed to take someone somewhere over time. Where the standalone session says *here is one thing to do today*, the journey says *here is where you will be in thirty days if you do these things consistently*.

Journeys are where creators on AlphaGlow build their most durable income. Listeners commit to a journey and engage with it repeatedly. Nova schedules and adapts the sessions based on each listener's progress.

## What happens after you publish

Once your content is live, Nova handles delivery. She recommends your program to listeners whose goals and patterns match what your program is designed for. You see completion rates, engagement signals, and NSVX flows in your creator dashboard in real time.

The platform does not reward follower counts or posting frequency. It rewards depth of engagement. That distinction matters.
