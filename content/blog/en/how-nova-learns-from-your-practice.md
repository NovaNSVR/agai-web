Most AI tools learn from everyone and apply that learning to you. Nova works differently. She builds a model of *you specifically* — your rhythms, your resistances, your breakthroughs — and uses that to deliver better support over time.

## What Nova tracks

Every interaction with Nova adds to her picture of you. Not surveillance — context. When you tell her you slept badly, that informs her session recommendation for today. When you complete a breathwork session at 7am three days in a row, she notices the pattern. When you skip two days and come back, she knows not to lecture you about it.

The signals she works with include:

- **Session completions and skips** — what you finish, what you abandon, and when
- **Check-in responses** — how you describe your energy, mood, and stress levels
- **Program engagement** — how deeply you engage with each creator's content
- **Time patterns** — when you tend to practice, and when you don't
- **Explicit feedback** — anything you rate or comment on

## Why this matters for creators

For creators, Nova's learning creates a flywheel. The better she understands each listener, the more precisely she can recommend the right program at the right moment. That means your sessions land with people who are genuinely ready for them — not just anyone scrolling a feed.

This is why completion rates on AlphaGlow run significantly higher than on open platforms. Nova isn't broadcasting your content. She's delivering it to the people most likely to benefit from it, at the moments when they're most likely to engage.

## Your data, always yours

Everything Nova knows about you is visible in your account dashboard. You can see her signals, edit them, and delete them. She works *with* your consent, not around it.

The goal is a companion who genuinely knows you — and earns that knowledge by being useful every day.
