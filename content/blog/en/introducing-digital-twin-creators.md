When a creator builds a Digital Twin on AlphaGlow, they are not building a chatbot. They are building an AI-powered version of their expertise — trained on their content, delivered in their voice, capable of having a genuine conversation about the work they do.

## What a Digital Twin can do

A creator's Digital Twin can answer questions their audience would normally send in a DM. It can walk someone through a breathwork technique, explain the science behind a nutritional protocol, or help a listener troubleshoot why a particular meditation practice isn't working for them.

The difference from a FAQ or a chatbot is depth. A Digital Twin has absorbed the creator's approach, vocabulary, and reasoning. It responds the way the creator would — because it was trained on how the creator actually communicates.

## How it's built

Digital Twins are built in Nova Studio using a combination of:

- **Creator content** — sessions, scripts, and written material the creator has produced
- **Voice synthesis** — the creator's actual voice, recreated by Cartesia
- **Structured knowledge** — the creator's approach, methodology, and key frameworks

The process takes a few hours, not weeks. Creators set boundaries on what their Digital Twin will and won't discuss. The AI stays in scope.

## What this means for creators economically

A Digital Twin earns while the creator sleeps. Every time a listener engages with a creator's Digital Twin, it registers as a session — and the creator earns NSVX from that engagement. Listeners who access a Digital Twin spend NSVX to do so, and 85% of that flows directly to the creator.

This is the model: build your expertise once, have it available to your audience permanently.
